What "organic"
actually means here.

No paraffin. No phthalates. No dyes. No shortcuts. Just a clean, even, 60-hour burn that leaves nothing behind but the scent.

Questions

Everything else

How long does each candle burn?

Approximately 60 hours per candle (9.5 oz / 270 g). Burned in our recommended 2–3 hour sessions, that's two to three months of daily ritual.

Are the scents safe around pets and kids?

Our blends use low, IFRA-compliant concentrations of pure essential oils and contain no synthetic fragrance or phthalates. As with any candle, burn in a ventilated room and keep out of reach.

Where are they made?

Every candle is hand-poured in our studio in small weekly batches of 200, then cured for 14 days so the scent throws fully from the first light.

What if I don't love it?

Our 30-day flame-out guarantee covers you: if you're not delighted, we refund you in full and you keep the candle. We'll also help you pick a better-suited scent.
