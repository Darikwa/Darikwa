# Niko the Ninja That Could! — Series Bible

> **This is the canonical reference for the whole franchise.** Characters,
> appearances, and the visual style bible below should stay consistent across
> every book, every illustration, and every future format. When in doubt,
> this document wins.

---

## 1. The Niko Universe

### The Village of Kageyama

A hidden village nestled between **mountains, forests, waterfalls and cherry
blossom valleys**.

Unlike most ninja villages, Kageyama values:

- **Kindness**
- **Perseverance**
- **Wisdom**
- **Community**

…over fighting.

> **Village motto:** *"A strong ninja helps others rise."*

This is the emotional engine of the whole series. Conflicts are resolved with
cleverness, kindness, and never-giving-up — never with realistic violence.

## 2. The Madziwa Family

### Niko Madziwa — *the hero*

- **Age:** 6 (in the main series; younger in the origin books)
- **Appearance:** Black hair, dark brown eyes, small build, navy ninja outfit,
  red sash
- **Personality:** Curious, determined, kind, clumsy, creative
- **Strength:** Never gives up
- **Weakness:** Compares himself to Akiko
- **Catchphrase:** *"Maybe I can't do it yet…"*

### Akiko Madziwa — *big sister & role model*

- **Age:** 12
- **Appearance:** Black hair, dark brown eyes, athletic build, taller than Niko
- **Personality:** Hard-working, protective, humble
- **Strength:** Disciplined
- **Weakness:** Can be too serious
- **Catchphrase:** *"Let's try one more time."*

### Daichi Madziwa — *father*

- **Age:** 40
- **Occupation:** Carpenter and village builder
- **Appearance:** Strong build, black hair with first signs of grey at the
  temples, warm smile
- **Personality:** Hard-working, patient, practical
- **Lesson he teaches:** *"Big things are built one piece at a time."*

### Hana Madziwa — *mother*

- **Age:** 38
- **Occupation:** Baker and herbal healer
- **Appearance:** Long black hair, kind eyes, usually wearing an apron
- **Personality:** Gentle, wise, encouraging
- **Lesson she teaches:** *"Kindness always travels further than anger."*

## 3. Mentors & Companions

### Sensei Willow

- **Age:** Nobody knows.
- **Appearance:** White beard, wooden staff, biscuits always hidden somewhere
  (the biscuit pouch)
- **Personality:** Funny, wise, mysterious
- **Catchphrase:** *"Interesting…"*

### Biscuit the Raccoon

- **Name:** Biscuit
- **Appearance:** Grey fur, bandit-mask markings, round belly
- **Personality:** Mischievous, clever, hungry
- **Favourite food:** Custard creams
- **Running joke:** Biscuit accidentally causes half of Niko's adventures.

## 4. Franchise Timeline

The series is designed so Niko **ages with the reader**.

| Stage | Niko's age | Themes & content |
|-------|-----------|------------------|
| **Origins** | 0–4 | Origin stories, family, first lessons, Akiko as role model |
| **Academy** | 5–7 | Ninja Academy, first friendships, first adventures |
| **Wider world** | 8–10 | Larger world, new villages, dragons, mysteries, festivals |
| **Leadership** | 11–13 | Leadership, mentoring younger ninjas, bigger challenges |

## 6. Visual Style Bible

> **This should never change.**

### Character locks

**Niko**
- Black hair (**always**)
- Dark brown eyes (**always**)
- Navy outfit
- Red sash

**Akiko**
- Black hair
- Dark brown eyes
- Taller than Niko
- Athletic

**Hana**
- Long black hair
- Warm smile

**Daichi**
- Black hair with grey at temples
- Strong build

**Sensei Willow**
- White beard
- Staff
- Biscuit pouch

### Art style

- Landscape format
- Storybook quality
- Studio Ghibli warmth
- Bluey character appeal
- Bright colours
- Soft lighting
- Cinematic compositions

### Never use

- ❌ Blonde hair for Niko
- ❌ Blue eyes for Niko
- ❌ Realistic violence
- ❌ Dark horror themes
- ❌ Hyper-realistic style

## 7. Tone & writing guidelines

- **Audience:** Read-aloud for ages 3–7; early independent readers can grow into it.
- **Rhythm:** Short sentences, gentle repetition, a refrain children can chant
  along with (Niko's *"Maybe I can't do it yet…"* and the village motto).
- **Emotional arc per book:** a small struggle → a kind nudge from family/mentor
  → Niko trying again → a warm, earned resolution.
- **Humour:** Biscuit the Raccoon provides physical comedy; Sensei Willow
  provides dry, twinkly wit.
- **Every book ends** with the feeling that effort and kindness matter more than
  being the best.
