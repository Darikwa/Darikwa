## PAGE-BY-PAGE

---

### Page 1 — The village between the mountains

> High between the mountains, hidden by forests and waterfalls and valleys full
> of cherry blossom, there was a village called **Kageyama**.
>
> In Kageyama, the strongest ninjas were not the ones who fought the hardest.
> They were the ones who helped the most.

> **Illustration brief**
> Wide establishing shot of Kageyama at golden hour. Tiered wooden rooftops,
> a waterfall, a red bridge, cherry trees in bloom, petals on the wind. Tiny
> villagers wave to one another. Soft lighting, cinematic depth, bright and
> inviting. Mood: safe, magical, welcoming.

---

### Page 2 — The Madziwa family

> In a cosy wooden house near the river lived the **Madziwa family**.
>
> **Daichi** the carpenter, who built half the village.
> **Hana** the baker, whose bread smelled like a hug.
> And **Akiko**, who was twelve, and already the bravest big sister you ever met.

> **Illustration brief**
> Interior of the Madziwa home, warm and lived-in. Daichi at a workbench with
> wood shavings curling around his feet; Hana pulling fresh bread from a clay
> oven, apron dusted with flour; Akiko mid-stretch in training clothes, ponytail
> swinging. Lanterns, hanging herbs, a kettle steaming. Keep character locks:
> all black hair, dark brown eyes; Daichi greying at temples.

---

### Page 3 — A little secret

> One spring morning, Hana smiled a special smile and pressed Akiko's hand to
> her tummy.
>
> "Soon," she whispered, "you're going to be a **big sister**."
>
> Akiko's heart went *thump-thump-thump* — half excited, half something she
> couldn't name yet.

> **Illustration brief**
> Close, tender two-shot of Hana and Akiko by a sunlit window, cherry blossom
> outside. Hana's warm smile; Akiko's face caught between a grin and worry.
> Soft morning light, gentle pastel palette. Intimate and quiet.

---

### Page 4 — Daichi builds, one piece at a time

> Daichi began to build a little wooden crib.
>
> A leg. Then a rail. Then a tiny carved star for the top.
>
> "Father, it's taking forever," said Akiko.
> Daichi just smiled. **"Big things are built one piece at a time."**

> **Illustration brief**
> Daichi in his workshop sanding a half-finished crib, a carved wooden star
> resting on the bench. Akiko watching, chin on hands. Warm sawdust light,
> tools on the wall, a window full of mountains. Show the crib clearly — it
> will reappear on page 15.

---

### Page 5 — Hana bakes for the whole village

> Hana baked and baked — honey buns and rice cakes and soft round loaves.
>
> "When a new baby comes to Kageyama," she said, "the **whole village** gets to
> celebrate. Kindness always travels further than anger."

> **Illustration brief**
> Hana's kitchen overflowing with baked treats cooling on every surface,
> steam rising. Through the open door, villagers arrive carrying lanterns and
> gifts. Biscuit the Raccoon sneaks a custard cream off the windowsill in the
> background (first appearance — keep him small and funny). Bright, abundant,
> cosy.

---

### Page 6 — Akiko's worry

> That night, Akiko lay awake.
>
> *What if I'm not a good big sister? What if the baby doesn't like me? What if
> I drop him?*
>
> She hugged her knees and whispered, "**Let's try one more time**" — the way
> she always did before something hard.

> **Illustration brief**
> Akiko curled up under a blanket in lamplight, moonlit window behind her, a
> single worried tear. Her training sash hangs on the wall. Tender, blue-toned
> night palette but still warm, never scary. Mood: real but gentle.

---

### Page 7 — The night the lanterns lit

> Then came the night.
>
> Hana's bread went cold on the table. Daichi ran for the healer. And one by
> one, all across Kageyama, the villagers lit their **lanterns** to wish the new
> baby a safe journey into the world.

> **Illustration brief**
> Sweeping night view of the whole village from above, every house glowing with
> a lantern, a soft golden river of light winding up the mountain to the Madziwa
> home. Stars, blossom, a hush of anticipation. Cinematic, awe-filled, hopeful.

---

### Page 8 — A tiny new cry

> And just before dawn…
>
> *"Waaah! Waaah!"*
>
> A tiny new cry — the smallest, fiercest sound — rang out across the quiet
> village.
>
> Akiko sat up. The baby had come.

> **Illustration brief**
> Soft, glowing bedroom at first light. Hana holding a tiny swaddled bundle,
> Daichi's strong hand resting gently on the blanket, both faces lit with tears
> of joy. Keep it warm and abstract — no medical detail. Akiko's hand pushing
> the door open, eyes huge. Dawn pinks and golds.

---

### Page 9 — "His name is Niko"

> Hana looked down at the little face — black hair, dark brown eyes blinking at
> the brand-new world.
>
> "His name," she said softly, "is **Niko**."
>
> Niko Madziwa. The newest, smallest ninja in all of Kageyama.

> **Illustration brief**
> Extreme tender close-up: baby Niko swaddled, a tuft of **black hair**, **dark
> brown eyes** open (CRITICAL character lock — never blonde, never blue). The
> family's faces ring the edges of frame, looking in. Soft focus, golden light,
> a single cherry petal drifting onto the blanket.

---

### Page 10 — The smallest ninja in Kageyama

> He was so *small*.
>
> Smaller than Daichi's hammer. Smaller than one of Hana's loaves. Smaller than
> Akiko's two hands together.
>
> "You can't walk yet," Akiko whispered. "You can't run, or climb, or jump."
> Niko gripped her finger anyway, tight and sure.

> **Illustration brief**
> Charming size-comparison spread: baby Niko beside a loaf of bread, a wooden
> hammer, and Akiko's cupped hands, all drawn to scale to show how tiny he is.
> Then a sweet inset of his tiny hand wrapped around Akiko's finger. Playful,
> bright, full of heart.

---

### Page 11 — Akiko holds her brother

> "Would you like to hold him?" asked Hana.
>
> Akiko's heart went *thump-thump-thump* again. She remembered her worry. Then
> she took a breath and held out her arms.
>
> And Niko fit. Just right. Like he had always belonged there.

> **Illustration brief**
> The emotional centre of the book. Akiko seated, cradling baby Niko with
> careful, glowing pride, Hana and Daichi watching with soft smiles. Warm
> lantern light haloing the pair. Akiko's worry from page 6 resolved on her
> face. Tender, luminous, the visual payoff of the family theme.

---

### Page 12 — Sensei Willow says "Interesting…"

> There was a knock. In the doorway stood old **Sensei Willow**, white beard
> almost to his belt, leaning on his wooden staff.
>
> He peered at the tiny baby for a long, long moment.
>
> "Hmm," he said at last. **"Interesting…"** And his eyes twinkled, as if he knew
> a secret about who this little one would become.

> **Illustration brief**
> Sensei Willow in the doorway at dawn — white beard, wooden staff, the famous
> biscuit pouch at his hip. He bends to peer at baby Niko with a knowing,
> twinkling smile. Backlit by morning light. Warm, mysterious, a touch of magic.
> Mood: a promise of adventures to come.

---

### Page 13 — Biscuit's first mischief

> Behind Sensei Willow, a round little shadow tiptoed in.
>
> **Biscuit the Raccoon** had smelled Hana's custard creams. He climbed, he
> wobbled, he *reached* — and down came the whole plate with a **CLATTER-CRASH!**
>
> Baby Niko's eyes went wide… and he **giggled**. His very first laugh.

> **Illustration brief**
> Comic action beat: Biscuit (grey fur, bandit mask, round belly) caught
> mid-tumble off a shelf, custard creams flying everywhere, plate clattering.
> The family startled-then-laughing. Most important: baby Niko mid-giggle.
> Bright, bouncy, slapstick energy — Bluey-style comedy. Establishes the running
> joke: Biscuit causes the chaos.

---

### Page 14 — A strong ninja helps others rise

> "He can't do anything yet," laughed Akiko, scooping up custard creams.
>
> Sensei Willow chuckled. "Not *yet*," he said. "But in Kageyama, we do not ask,
> *is the ninja strong?* We ask, *does the ninja help others rise?*"
>
> "And this one," said Hana, kissing Niko's head, "already made us all rise out
> of bed with joy."

> **Illustration brief**
> The whole cast together in the cosy main room at full morning light: Hana and
> baby Niko at centre, Daichi, Akiko, Sensei Willow, and a sheepish Biscuit
> holding a single rescued custard cream. A small wall scroll reads the village
> motto: *"A strong ninja helps others rise."* Group warmth, everyone connected,
> the family theme made whole.

---

### Page 15 — Goodnight, little Niko

> That night, Daichi laid Niko in the little wooden crib with the carved star on
> top — finished, at last, one piece at a time.
>
> One day Niko would walk. One day he would run, and climb, and jump, and try,
> and *try again*.
>
> But tonight he was small, and safe, and surrounded by all the people who loved
> him.
>
> "Goodnight, little Niko," whispered Akiko. "Tomorrow, we begin."

> **Illustration brief**
> Quiet closing image: baby Niko asleep in the finished crib (the carved star
> from page 4 glints on top), moonlight and lantern glow mixing. The family
> silhouetted in the doorway, Akiko's hand on the frame, Biscuit curled asleep
> at the crib's foot. Mirrors the cover's warmth. Peaceful, full-circle, a
> gentle promise of Book 2. Final petal drifting down.
