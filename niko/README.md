# Niko the Ninja That Could! — Franchise

A children's book franchise about a small, clumsy, big-hearted ninja named
**Niko Madziwa** who lives in the hidden village of **Kageyama**, where a strong
ninja is measured not by how hard they fight, but by how well they help others
rise.

> **Village motto:** *"A strong ninja helps others rise."*

The series is built to **grow with the reader**. Children who meet Niko at age
5–6 can follow him as he ages from a newborn through to a young leader at 13.

## Franchise timeline at a glance

| Reader age | Niko's age | Story focus |
|------------|-----------|-------------|
| 0–4 | Origin → toddler | Family, first lessons, Akiko as role model |
| 5–7 | Ninja Academy | First friendships, first adventures |
| 8–10 | Wider world | New villages, dragons, mysteries, festivals |
| 11–13 | Leadership | Mentoring younger ninjas, bigger challenges |

## Format & production notes

- **Format:** Landscape picture book, 15 interior pages.
- **Art direction:** Storybook quality with Studio Ghibli warmth and Bluey
  character appeal — bright colours, soft lighting, cinematic compositions.
- **Consistency:** Every illustration must follow the
  [Visual Style Bible](series-bible.md#visual-style-bible). Niko **always** has
  black hair and dark brown eyes. No exceptions.
