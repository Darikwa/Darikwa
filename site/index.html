I

Two decades on the shop floor of British retail.

From the warehouses of Dreams to the data engines of Tesco and the storefront of a premium London fashion brand, I have spent my career where commerce meets code — translating between the finance office, the stockroom and the engineering team.

That fluency is the point. Most technology fails commercially not because the code is bad, but because nobody in the room speaks all three languages. Darikwa exists to be that person — and now brings applied AI into the conversation: not as a buzzword, but as a working material with a margin attached.

IV

Questions, answered.

What does Darikwa do?

Darikwa designs and builds commercial technology for ambitious businesses: accounting and inventory platforms, e-commerce builds and migrations, end-to-end process mapping, and custom web applications — increasingly with AI and automation at the core.

Who is behind Darikwa?

Edward Madziwa, a UK-based commercial technology consultant with two decades across British retail, including Dreams, Tesco and a premium London fashion brand.

How does Darikwa use AI?

As an engineering material, not a gimmick: LLM integrations inside finance and operations workflows, generative tooling, and automations that remove repetitive work — always measured against a commercial outcome.

Is Darikwa taking on new projects?

Yes — currently open for new projects, from focused MVPs and dashboards to full platforms and e-commerce replatforming.
